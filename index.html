About

I turn complex requirements into reliable, maintainable Android apps — from architecture and modularization to background processing, native integrations, and testing.

Over 8+ years I've designed, developed, tested, and delivered Android across smartphones, tablets, enterprise, client-server, IoT, robotics, payments, and KYC. I'm an expert in Kotlin and Java with deep, hands-on work in the Android SDK, Jetpack Compose, MVVM/MVP/MVC, Clean Architecture, Coroutines & Flow, RxJava, WorkManager, AIDL/Binder, and multi-module development.

My integration toolkit spans REST & GraphQL, Retrofit/OkHttp, Firebase, Room/SQLite, Google Maps & Location, Camera/CameraX/Camera2, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, sensors, TensorFlow, JNI/NDK, OAuth, and payment gateways — backed by real testing (JUnit, Mockito, Espresso, UI Automator) and CI/CD with Jenkins & CircleCI. I care about legacy modernization, performance, and secure, scalable delivery.
